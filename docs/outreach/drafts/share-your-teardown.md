# DRAFT — FOR HUMAN REVIEW — "share your teardown" pitch

> Purpose: a short, reusable pitch that channels interested people to the
> Teardown Report issue template. Use it as a closing paragraph in posts, a
> standalone comment where someone describes their own teardown-ish work, or
> the basis of a pinned Discussion. Adjust the intro line per context.

## The pitch

If you've ever audited what your team actually uses in a SaaS product — or
you're willing to run one teardown on a tenant you administer — I'd love to
have the result in the open corpus. There's a structured issue form for it:

https://github.com/chrbailey/saas-rebuild/issues/new?template=teardown-report.yml

Two things to know before you post:

1. **It's structure only, sanitized by design.** The form asks for verdict
   counts, the workflows that actually earn the subscription, coverage blind
   spots, and preservation status — never record contents, exports, names,
   real-data screenshots, or internal URLs. Ranges are fine ("5–10 users").
   If naming the app is uncomfortable, name just the category ("property
   management suite"). There's a required sanitization checkbox, and I mean
   it.
2. **Gaps are the most useful part.** The "what this skill missed" field
   exists because real teardowns finding real holes is how the pipeline
   improves. A report that says "the protocol had no step for X" is worth
   more to me than a tidy success story.

Even a partial teardown — one module, one role, a 30-day window — is
publishable through the form as long as you state the coverage honestly.
The contracts are built to record uncertainty, not hide it.

## Shorter variant (for tight comment threads)

I collect sanitized teardown reports through a structured issue form —
verdict counts, KEEP sets, coverage limits, what the pipeline missed; never
record contents or names. If you run one on a tenant you administer, I'd
genuinely like the result, especially the gaps:
https://github.com/chrbailey/saas-rebuild/issues/new?template=teardown-report.yml
